Selected publications

Evidence, published.

My work spans AI-enabled therapeutics, molecular representation, biomedical language models and generative biological imaging.

Complete list on Google Scholar ↗
  1. 2026

    DeepDrugDiscovery identifies blood-brain barrier permeable autophagy enhancers for Alzheimer’s disease

    Co-first author · Nature Biomedical Engineering

    An integrated, mechanism-aware virtual-screening platform that searched more than 1.16 million compounds and identified mTOR-independent autophagy enhancers with blood-brain-barrier permeability and neuroprotective potential.

    ↗
  2. 2024

    PharmaBench: Enhancing ADMET benchmarks with large language models

    Co-first author · Scientific Data

    A multi-agent LLM pipeline extracted experimental conditions from 14,401 bioassays and helped assemble eleven open ADMET datasets containing 52,482 curated entries for realistic model evaluation.

    ↗
  3. 2025

    Artificial immunofluorescence in a flash: Rapid synthetic imaging from brightfield through residual diffusion

    Neurocomputing · CellResDM

    A residual-diffusion model synthesises immunofluorescence images and cell-segmentation masks directly from brightfield microscopy, delivering substantially faster inference while preserving image and segmentation quality.

    ↗
  4. 2024

    How good are current pocket-based 3D generative models?

    Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling · POKMOL-3D

    A benchmark spanning 32 protein targets, known active compounds and complementary 2D and 3D metrics to reveal where pocket-conditioned molecular generators succeed - and where they still fail.

    ↗
  5. 2023

    An extensive benchmark study on biomedical text generation and mining with ChatGPT

    Bioinformatics

    A broad evaluation of ChatGPT across biomedical entity recognition, relation extraction, semantic similarity, question answering and document classification, mapping both its versatility and its limits.

    ↗
  6. 2022

    Amelioration of Alzheimer’s disease pathology by mitophagy inducers identified via machine learning and a cross-species workflow

    Nature Biomedical Engineering

    Machine learning and experimental validation across cells, nematodes and rodents identified kaempferol and rhapontigenin as potent mitophagy inducers that improved neuronal function and Alzheimer’s disease phenotypes.
